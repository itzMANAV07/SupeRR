How to read Rank stability and Likely rank
Rank stability counts how many of five re-runs of the model kept the player in the top 10: Solid = all 5, Moderate = 3 or 4, Uncertain = 0 to 2. It describes how stable the data is, not how good the player is.
Likely rank is where the player would finish in about 8 out of 10 bootstrap re-runs. A narrow range (1 to 12) means the position is solid; a wide range (3 to 62) means it could change a lot.
In short: trust the Solid names and treat the others as shortlist options. Players marked “No IPL data” have no IPL pressure balls.
